Asciimoji

Privacy

Last updated November 13, 2026.

Asciimoji is a tap-to-copy collection of ascii emoji. This policy covers the Asciimoji app and the website at asciimoji.app.

Emoji you copy

Tapping an emoji copies it to the clipboard on your device so you can paste it somewhere else. That text stays on your device. Asciimoji does not receive, store, or transmit the emoji you copy.

Information we collect

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or the contents of your clipboard.

When the app opens, it can ask Expo whether a published update is available. That check exists so the app can download an update. It is not used to build a profile of you.

This website does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, or cookies. It is a static site hosted on Render. Render may keep ordinary connection logs, such as an IP address and the page requested, in order to operate the site.

Other companies

Apple distributes the iOS app through the App Store. Your App Store account is covered by Apple’s privacy policy. Expo hosts published updates of the app.

Children

The app and this website do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
